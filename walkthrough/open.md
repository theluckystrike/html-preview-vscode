# Open the preview

1. Open an `.html` file.
2. Click the preview icon at the top right of the editor, or press `Cmd+K V` (`Ctrl+K V` on Windows and Linux).

The preview opens beside your code. Your `.html` file stays a normal text editor.

The preview updates as you type. Saved changes to linked CSS, JS and images show at once, and the scroll position stays where it was.
